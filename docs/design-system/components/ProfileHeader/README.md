ProfileHeader is the wall behind the whole profile: a taped polaroid avatar, the slug, the name in poster type, one handwritten tagline, a short bio, a row of category stickers with counts and the main actions.

Provide the avatar image in `mq-art` inside `mq-polaroid` with a short caption, the slug, display name, optional tagline, bio, the counts per category, the actions "Copiar link" (primary), "Ver quarto" and share, and the links to the owner's profiles on other platforms (`mq-profile__links` with one `mq-link` per platform: the platform name and the `external-link` icon, opening in a new tab, in a `nav` labelled "<nome> em outras plataformas"). The rail along the bottom (`border`) is where the shelves of cards start. On narrow screens it stacks and the name drops to 44px.

Do: keep one handwritten element beyond the photo caption (the tagline); keep the platform links as quiet text in `ink-muted`, never buttons or stickers (stickers mean a category). Do not: add more than three actions, put the bio in handwriting, or show raw numbers without their sticker.
